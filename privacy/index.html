Privacy notice

How we use your information

Last updated 14 November 2026

Who we are

This website is run by Michael Balogun trading as You Should Travel Well, an Independent Travel Advisor with InteleTravel (ABTA P7384). For anything about your data, email info@youshouldtravelwell.com.

What we collect

  • Trip enquiries: your name, email, optional phone number and the trip details you give us.
  • Creator enquiries: your name, email, social platform, handle, follower range, whether you've been an InteleTravel advisor and any message.
  • Newsletter sign-ups: your email address.

Why we use it

To reply to you and help with what you asked about, and to send the newsletter if you signed up. We rely on your consent, which you give when you tick the box on a form, and you can withdraw it at any time.

Who sees it

Form messages are sent to our email inbox (hosted by Titan) and a backup copy is kept on our web host (Hostinger). If you go ahead with a booking, the details needed are passed to InteleTravel and the travel company you book with, under their own privacy policies. We don't sell your information.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiries for up to two years after we last hear from you, then delete them. Newsletter addresses are kept until you unsubscribe.

Fonts and links

This site loads its fonts from Google Fonts, which means your browser connects to Google's servers. We don't use advertising or tracking cookies. Our booking site is run by InteleTravel and has its own privacy policy.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your information, ask us to correct or delete it, or object to how we use it. Email info@youshouldtravelwell.com. If you're unhappy with how we've handled your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

If you book through us, we earn commission from the travel company. It doesn't change the price you pay.
