Santorini, Greece

Santorini in 2018: an e-bike sunset tour and a caldera sailing trip

Visited July 2018Our rating: 5/5We paid for this trip ourselves

Two of the best ways to see the island: by e-bike, and from the water.

Good for: Active couples and friends

eBike Sunset Tour (our rating: 5/5)

We took the Santorini eBike Sunset Tour, and it's a wonderful way to explore the island. You see beautiful scenery that you simply can't reach by car.

It is a bit of a workout: about 17 miles of cycling with some walking, so it's not one for a lazy day. The e-bikes make it much easier, and they're great fun. Our guide was incredibly knowledgeable and very kind.

Caldera sailing tour with BBQ lunch (our rating: 4/5)

A wonderful day out. The crew were excellent: friendly, helpful, happy to take photos and quick to keep the drinks coming. Our captain was knowledgeable, the journey was smooth and lunch on board was delicious.

Book the Triton boat if you can. It takes fewer than ten people, so it can get closer to the shore. Most of the stops are for swimming, so it suits strong swimmers best, though you'll still enjoy it if you're not.

The one downside was the transfer: our driver nearly reversed into the sea. Santorini driving is a little crazy.

Our verdict

Do both. The e-bike tour for the views you can’t reach by car, and the sailing trip for swimming in the caldera. Bring comfortable shoes and swimwear.

This review reflects our own visit in July 2018. Hotels, prices and opening times change, so we check the latest details before you book.
