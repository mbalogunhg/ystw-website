New Orleans, USA

New Orleans: skip Bourbon Street, head for Frenchmen Street

Visited January 2014Our rating: 5/5We paid for this trip ourselves

Live jazz, a freezing swamp tour and very good shrimp.

Good for: Music lovers and food lovers

Where we stayed

Homewood Suites by Hilton: simple, comfortable and well placed for exploring. We barely spent time there, and had no complaints.

What we did

New Orleans is the home of jazz, and two streets show its two sides. Bourbon Street is loud and rowdy, full of bars and clubs; fun if that's what you're after. Frenchmen Street is where the music is. Wander from bar to bar and listen to brilliant live jazz; we especially liked the Blue Nile and the Spotted Cat.

We also did a swamp tour on an airboat. Dress warmly, because it's cold and noisy in January. We didn't see any alligators, but there was still plenty to see and it was a fun day out.

Where we ate

Seafood from the Gulf of Mexico. You can't go far wrong with the shrimp, and try a gumbo.

What we missed

A plantation tour and one of the city's famous ghost tours.

Our verdict

Go for the music, and spend your evenings on Frenchmen Street.

This review reflects our own visit in January 2014. Hotels, prices and opening times change, so we check the latest details before you book.
