Las Vegas, USA

There’s more to Las Vegas: the Grand Canyon West and Hoover Dam day tour

Visited April 2024Our rating: 4/5We paid for this trip ourselves

An early start and a long drive, but a brilliant day out from the Strip, with a superb guide.

Good for: Anyone who wants to see more than the casinos

The tour

We took the Grand Canyon West and Hoover Dam tour with SWEETours, which starts with breakfast at IHOP. It's an early start, but that's what lets you take in all the views. The drive from Las Vegas is very scenic, so have your camera ready.

Our guide was a gem: so knowledgeable, and full of great tips and suggestions. There's far more to Las Vegas than gambling and nightlife, and this tour is one of the best ways to see it.

What to bring

A charged phone or camera and a power bank, plus a mains charger for the coach; you'll take a lot of photos and videos, so keep your phone on airplane mode to save battery. Snacks, and a book or tablet, because it's a long drive. Comfortable trainers, ideally a dark pair. An eye mask for a nap on the way back. And some cash to tip your guide.

Our verdict

Worth the early alarm. Go prepared for a long day on the coach and you’ll love it.

This review reflects our own visit in April 2024. Hotels, prices and opening times change, so we check the latest details before you book.
