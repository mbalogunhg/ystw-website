Mykonos, Greece

Solymar, Mykonos: a beach club worth the day out

Visited September 2021Our rating: 4/5We paid for this trip ourselves

Crystal-clear water, sunbeds right at the water’s edge, and a lunch we’d go back for.

Good for: Couples and friends looking for a beach day with a party later on

The beach club

Solymar sits on Kalo Livadi beach. The setting is lovely and the water is crystal clear and perfect for swimming. The best thing about it is that you can have a sunbed right by the water, so you can go in for a dip and be back on your day bed in seconds.

Your host looks after you all day, and we'd recommend having lunch at the restaurant: the food is brilliant. If you stay for the entertainment, it gets the party going.

Our verdict

Not the cheapest, but well worth the day out. We’ll be back.

This review reflects our own visit in September 2021. Hotels, prices and opening times change, so we check the latest details before you book.
