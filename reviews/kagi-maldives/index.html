Kagi Maldives: great Asian food, too much upselling

Visited February 2024We paid for this trip ourselves

A beautiful island with excellent food and good service, let down by constant pressure to spend more.

Good for: Couples who love Asian food

Getting there

When you land at Velana International Airport in Malé, the resort's team should meet you, help with your bags and take you to the domestic terminal for the seaplane. Seaplanes only fly in daylight, so choose a flight that lands during the day; otherwise you'll transfer by speedboat. Kagi has use of a lounge while you wait.

Where we ate

If you love Asian food, you'll love Kagi. At the Spa Corner we liked the green and watermelon-berry juices, the udon and the gyoza, which come with a delicious sauce, though oddly the wrappers changed from day to day. At Ke-un, the empanadas, tacos, choo chee goong (prawns) and the beef massaman curry were all highlights.

The downside

We often felt the staff were tasked with upselling: a special dinner here, a paid water activity there, and masseuses suggesting a massage while we were eating lunch at the Spa Corner. Nobody wants a sales pitch during a meal. You spend a lot to get to the Maldives, and the last thing you want is to be asked to spend more once you're there. To be fair, the customer service is very good, and that just about outweighs it.

The air conditioning in our room was hard to keep at a steady temperature.

Leaving

Malé airport was a frustrating experience on the way home. Everyone goes through a security check before bag drop, which creates a bottleneck, and then a second security check after immigration. Allow plenty of time.

Our verdict

Go for the food and the island, book flights that land in daylight, and be ready to say no thank you.

This review reflects our own visit in February 2024. Hotels, prices and opening times change, so we check the latest details before you book.
