Mauritius, Mauritius

Mauritius: Long Beach Hotel, all-inclusive and very active

Visited February 2016Our rating: 5/5We paid for this trip ourselves

Eight nights on the island’s longest beach, with more free activities than we could fit in.

Good for: Couples and families who want a beach holiday with plenty to do

Where we stayed

Long Beach Hotel & Spa sits on the longest beach in Mauritius, with lovely service, good food and lots to keep you busy. Go all-inclusive; you won't regret it.

What we did

We could easily have spent all eight nights in the hotel, which is a short walk from the rooms to the private beach. If lying still isn't your thing, the hotel has snorkelling, archery, golf, taekwondo, tennis, volleyball, cycling, a climbing wall, windsurfing, kayaking and more.

Outside the hotel, Casela Nature Park makes a full day out, and we got around parts of it on Segways. For something faster, the Seakart is a cross between a jet ski and a speedboat, and racing around the Indian Ocean on one was brilliant fun.

Where we ate

Almost entirely at the hotel, and we came home a few pounds heavier. There are several à la carte restaurants with different cuisines, so book ahead, and ask for dinner on the beach if you can.

What we missed

Black River Gorges National Park and the Seven Coloured Earth at Chamarel. We'll be back.

Our verdict

A beach holiday with something for everyone. Go all-inclusive and book the restaurants early.

This review reflects our own visit in February 2016. Hotels, prices and opening times change, so we check the latest details before you book.
