Lake Como, Italy

Lake Como: the trip that went wrong, and the lunch that saved it

Visited April 2016Our rating: 4/5We paid for this trip ourselves

A delayed flight, a broken-down hire car and a €200 taxi. But also a wonderful lunch at Grand Hotel Tremezzo.

Good for: Anyone planning to drive around the lake (read this first)

Where we stayed

New Lake View Apartments in Tremezzo were adequate for one night and handy for exploring, but the photos looked much better than the reality, and it was very hard to find in the dark. The roads above the lake are extremely narrow; hire the smallest car you can. A Fiat 500 is about right.

What happened

Our flight into Milan was delayed, so by the time we had collected the hire car and driven to the lake it was late at night, and we had lost a day of a two-day trip. The next morning, our brand-new Fiat 500 hire car broke down. Arranging recovery in Italian with a translation app was an experience, and because it was a Saturday, we were told we'd have to take a taxi all the way back to Malpensa airport for a replacement car. That cost about €200.

Where we ate

Before that taxi, we asked the recovery driver to drop us at Grand Hotel Tremezzo, about a mile from where we broke down, for lunch. The restaurant overlooks the lake and, even on a grey day, the view was beautiful. Food, service and décor were all lovely, and reception kindly arranged our taxi, which aren't easy to find around the lake.

What we missed

Almost everything: a speedboat hire, a motorbike tour and the restaurants along the lake. We'll be back.

Our verdict

Not Como’s fault. Allow more time than you think, drive something small, and have a plan B for the airport transfer.

This review reflects our own visit in April 2016. Hotels, prices and opening times change, so we check the latest details before you book.
