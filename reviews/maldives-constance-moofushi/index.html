Maldives, Maldives

Constance Moofushi, Maldives: no shoes required

Visited August 2016Our rating: 5/5We paid for this trip ourselves

A flawless all-inclusive island resort. And an honest take on whether a water villa is worth it.

Good for: Honeymooners, couples and snorkellers

Where we stayed

Constance Moofushi was flawless. When you step off the seaplane, they tell you to take your shoes off because you won't need them, and they're right: everything is sand or wooden decking.

We stayed in a water villa, which is worth doing for the novelty. But honestly, we think they're overhyped, at least here. The main difference from the beach villas is the steps down into the sea, which is brilliant for snorkelling, but the room and the shower were noticeably smaller.

The staff live on the island, so there's always someone to help, including walking you back to your villa after a night on the dance floor with the resident DJ.

What we did

The Maldives is about relaxing and indulging, and most people we met were on honeymoon. There's snorkelling, windsurfing, kayaking, glass-bottom boats and fishing. We mostly snorkelled and relaxed, which is exactly what we came for.

Where we ate

The food was excellent, with a themed dinner in the main restaurant every night and a good wine list. Go all-inclusive. Our only complaint: breakfast finished at around 9.30 to 10am, which is early after a late night.

What we'd do differently

Go in a drier season. We visited in August and would have liked more sunny days.

Our verdict

A superb resort in every way. Choose a beach villa for more space, and go all-inclusive.

This review reflects our own visit in August 2016. Hotels, prices and opening times change, so we check the latest details before you book.
