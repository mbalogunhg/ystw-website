Turin, Italy

Principi di Piemonte, Turin: first-class service, and great with a baby

Visited May 2025Our rating: 5/5We paid for this trip ourselves

A central hotel where nothing is too much trouble.

Good for: Families and city-break couples

Where we stayed

Principi di Piemonte is in a perfect, central spot, so you can walk to plenty of restaurants. What really makes it stand out is the service: nothing is too much trouble, and everything is done with a smile.

We stayed in a suite, which was very spacious. We told the hotel we were travelling with a baby and they quickly provided a baby bath and a cot, and anything else we needed they were on hand to sort out.

Our verdict

We can’t speak highly enough of it. Whenever we’re in Turin, this is where we’ll stay.

This review reflects our own visit in May 2025. Hotels, prices and opening times change, so we check the latest details before you book.
