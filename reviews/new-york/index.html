New York, USA

New York in a long weekend: landmarks and rooftops

Visited July 2015Our rating: 5/5We paid for this trip ourselves

The big sights, two of the city’s best rooftops, and a list of everything we still need to see.

Good for: First-time visitors and city-break lovers

Where we stayed

The Carlton Hotel, Autograph Collection, was a comfortable base in a good location. Fine for a weekend; for a longer stay we'd choose somewhere else.

What we did

As many landmarks as we could: the Empire State Building, the 9/11 Memorial, Brooklyn Bridge, Times Square, Fifth Avenue and Wall Street. The 9/11 Memorial is the one that stays with you; photos don't do it justice. Walking Brooklyn Bridge is a bit of a hike, so next time we'll cycle it. Times Square is a sensory overload, so find a seat and take it in.

New York does rooftops brilliantly. 230 Fifth has an exceptional view of the Empire State Building lit up at night, a relaxed terrace and a club inside. The Standard in the Meatpacking District has great views, but gets busy fast, so arrive early. Afterwards, wander the Meatpacking District for dinner.

Where we ate

Nothing stood out, but nothing disappointed either. Good pizza, ribs and burgers; it's hard to go far wrong.

What we missed

One World Observatory, Madison Square Garden, the Statue of Liberty and Central Park. Next time.

Our verdict

One of the best cities in the world. Give it more than a weekend.

This review reflects our own visit in July 2015. Hotels, prices and opening times change, so we check the latest details before you book.
