Cape Town, South Africa

Cape Town in three nights: Table Mountain, the Winelands and a lot of good food

Visited August 2016Our rating: 5/5We paid for this trip ourselves

A superb hotel, a city packed with things to do, and restaurants that are excellent value from the UK.

Good for: Couples, foodies and first-time visitors to South Africa

Where we stayed

The One&Only Cape Town: superb grounds, service and food, with a Nobu restaurant inside. We have no complaints at all.

What we did

Table Mountain came first, because the view of it from the hotel is hard to ignore. Book your tickets in advance to skip the queue. The views over the Cape are worth every minute.

No trip to South Africa is complete without the Winelands. We visited Stellenbosch on a private tour, which is good value and means you visit several vineyards in a more personal way.

Where we ate

Eating out in Cape Town is good value when you're converting from pounds. Meat lovers are well looked after and there's plenty of seafood too. We ate at NV-80, Gold, Gordon's and Nobu, and all were excellent. Gordon's was our favourite, so good we went twice; the service was slow, but the food made up for it.

What we missed

Robben Island. We had booked it, but the tour was cancelled because of bad weather. Three nights isn't enough for Cape Town, and we'll be back.

Our verdict

Give Cape Town longer than three nights, and book Table Mountain and Robben Island early.

This review reflects our own visit in August 2016. Hotels, prices and opening times change, so we check the latest details before you book.
