Chicago, USA

Chicago: a weekend with friends, and the best deep-dish pizza

Visited July 2016Our rating: 4/5We paid for this trip ourselves

A great hotel by the lake, a food festival we stumbled into, and pizza we still think about.

Good for: Group weekends and food lovers

Where we stayed

W Chicago Lakeshore sits across from Lake Michigan; ask for a room with a view. Lovely décor, helpful staff and very nice rooms.

What we did

This was a weekend away with friends, so culture wasn't top of the list. By luck we arrived during Taste of Chicago, a festival of food, drink and music, and spent a happy day there. We also stopped at Cloud Gate, ‘the Bean’, for the obligatory photos. Downtown has a great nightlife.

Where we ate

Giordano's, for proper Chicago deep-dish pizza. Book ahead because the wait can be long, but it's worth it. Six hungry people couldn't finish their orders.

Next time

Chicago's jazz scene, and a bit more culture.

Our verdict

A brilliant city for a weekend. Stay by the lake and book Giordano’s.

This review reflects our own visit in July 2016. Hotels, prices and opening times change, so we check the latest details before you book.
