Jamaica, Jamaica

Excellence Oyster Bay, Jamaica: great facilities and brilliant staff, but the food let it down

Visited February 2019Our rating: 4/5We paid for this trip ourselves

Eight nights near Falmouth to celebrate our birthday. Book the Excellence Club, and choose your restaurants carefully.

Good for: Couples celebrating something

Overall

We spent eight nights at Excellence Oyster Bay, near Falmouth, to celebrate our birthday, and overall we'd give it 4 out of 5. We had an amazing time, and many of the staff went out of their way to make the trip special. We chose it because we'd loved a stay at Excellence in Mexico the year before; Oyster Bay didn't quite match it, but it had only been open for just over a year.

Facilities (5/5)

Excellent. Go for the Excellence Club: there are great pools and beaches, plenty of restaurants and private areas for Club guests. There's also lots going on, especially the gym classes and the games room.

Our room (4/5)

In the Club we had a very spacious room with a big TV, a huge walk-in shower and a jacuzzi. The lighting was poorly designed, and there were a lot of mosquitoes, made worse when doors were left open during turndown. Pack repellent, or ask for plug-ins.

We'd recommend the Club Junior Suite. It's good value, close to the spa and Magna restaurant, and away from the main pool, which we suspect would be noisier.

Food (2/5)

Very hit and miss. Magna, Spice (the hibachi table), Chez Isabelle and The Kitchen Table were all pretty good. The Lobster House and Flavour Market were poor, and oddly there was no lobster at the Lobster House when we visited. Room service was very disappointing. Service in the restaurants was slow for most of our stay, though it improved towards the end.

Service (3.5/5)

The staff are lovely and always on hand to help, and several of them made our week. We'd love to see the resort let its team be themselves and bring in more genuine Jamaican culture and food.

The Michael Jackson tribute show was the best night of the week.

Our verdict

A lovely resort for a celebration, with superb facilities and staff. Book the Club, pick the better restaurants, and pack mosquito repellent.

This review reflects our own visit in February 2019. Hotels, prices and opening times change, so we check the latest details before you book.
