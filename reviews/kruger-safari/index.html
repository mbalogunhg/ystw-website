Kruger, South Africa

Kruger: three days of safari, and the Big Five

Visited August 2016Our rating: 5/5We paid for this trip ourselves

A basic lodge, 4.30am starts and freezing mornings, all worth it for the wildlife.

Good for: First-time safari-goers and anyone who likes an early start

Where we stayed

Kruger Park Lodge was an OK base and not much more. The grounds were nice, but the lodges were basic and the food was poor. We probably wouldn't stay again. If you do, the lodges have full kitchens and there are supermarkets nearby, so self-catering is the better option. You'll also need your own car; there's no public transport to speak of.

The safari

Safari drives start early. We knew that, but we weren't ready for a 4.30am start, followed by a 45-minute drive to the national park in an open jeep. South African winter mornings and nights are freezing. Don't wear shorts. We learned that once.

We told our guide we'd come for the Big Five and he laughed. Then we spotted a herd of elephants almost straight away, and another group radioed in a sighting of cheetahs, which our guide told us are rare in the park. Soon we were driving alongside three of them, completely unbothered by the cars following them. By the end of three days we had seen the Big Five and much more, from giraffes and zebras to hippos, and even a lion hunting an impala.

The night game drive was memorable for the wrong reasons. Our guide was new, didn't know the routes well and reversed into a tree, and someone in our group shone a torch straight into a rhino's eyes. Never do that. We moved the torch away quickly, and thankfully it didn't charge.

Other things we did

The Tree Top Trail is hard work and great fun if you're competitive. Quad biking on the mountain trails is a thrill, with some steep drops along the way.

Where we ate

Mostly at the lodge, which was the weak point of the trip. Self-cater if you can.

Our verdict

Spectacular. Choose a better lodge than we did, pack warm layers, and the wildlife does the rest. Next stop: the Great Migration.

This review reflects our own visit in August 2016. Hotels, prices and opening times change, so we check the latest details before you book.
