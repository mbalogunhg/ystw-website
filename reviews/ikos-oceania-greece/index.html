Halkidiki, Greece

Ikos Oceania, Greece: a family all-inclusive that lives up to its name

Visited May 2015Our rating: 4/5We paid for this trip ourselves

First-class service, beautiful grounds and lots to do. Book the à la carte restaurants before you go.

Good for: Families and groups who want to stay put and relax

Where we stayed

Ikos Oceania has first-class service from start to finish, picturesque grounds and spacious, comfortable rooms. Ask for a room closer to the beach, as the walk down is a bit of a trek. Rooms higher up do get sunset views over the Aegean, and the porters are happy to drive you around.

There are three pools, one of them indoors, plus a football pitch, a basketball court, bikes and plenty of scheduled activities. We'd recommend the Deluxe package, which adds a better room-service menu and access to a private pool and bar.

What we did

Not much, and that was the point. This was a family break, so we stayed on the beach and joined in everything going. Most activities are free, though some on the beach, such as jet skis, cost extra. We left the resort only once, for a morning in town.

Where we ate

There are four à la carte restaurants, a beach bar and a buffet. Book the à la carte restaurants before you travel because spaces are very limited, and we wished the hotel booked every guest into each one at least once. The Asian restaurant was the best by far. The buffet, where you'll eat most meals, was a let-down. The hotel also runs dinners out at a local Greek restaurant.

Our verdict

A lovely, well-run family resort. Book the restaurants early and ask for a room near the beach.

This review reflects our own visit in May 2015. Hotels, prices and opening times change, so we check the latest details before you book.
