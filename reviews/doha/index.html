Doha, Qatar

Doha: a beautiful bar at Raffles, and a resort that fell short

Visited February 2023We paid for this trip ourselves

A week in Qatar for our birthdays: a lovely evening at Raffles Doha, and an island resort we would not go back to.

Good for: Couples, with careful choice of hotel

Raffles Doha (our rating: 4/5)

Raffles Doha in Lusail has one of the most exceptional hotel lobbies you'll ever see. We went to the Acoustic Music Penthouse for pre-dinner drinks: excellent service, lovely décor, stunning views over Doha and very good nibbles. We went on a Thursday and it was very quiet, and the music stopped partway through the evening, which was odd. Otherwise, a perfect spot before dinner.

Banana Island Resort Doha by Anantara (our rating: 2/5)

We have stayed in a number of five-star resorts, and this one fell well short. We had booked a king room for a seven-night stay, but on arrival we were given a twin room with the beds pushed together, and it took over an hour to arrange. The resort was busy for Valentine's Day, and the only way to guarantee a proper double bed was to pay to upgrade to a suite. When a hotel makes a mistake, we'd expect it to put it right at no cost.

The food in the restaurants was poor, and in-room dining was the best option. Half board was poor value: a limited menu, no soft drinks included and supplements on most things, so pay as you go instead. The minibar wasn't included either, at close to $10 for a chocolate bar. The wifi barely worked, and turndown service didn't include a proper clean. Staff asked for card payment on every order even though we'd given an $800 pre-authorisation on arrival. And check whether an activity costs extra before you do it.

Our verdict

Raffles Doha is worth a visit for drinks. For an island stay, we would choose somewhere else.

This review reflects our own visit in February 2023. Hotels, prices and opening times change, so we check the latest details before you book.
