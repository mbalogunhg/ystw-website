The question

How much travel goes through you in a year?

The trips you take, the family holiday you always end up organising, the followers who message asking where to stay. As a creator you probably already earn when people book through your links. But when you plan and book the trip yourself, that work usually earns you nothing.

Questions

What creators usually ask first.

Is this an MLM?

InteleTravel is a host travel agency: advisors earn commission from travel companies on the bookings they make. It also has a sponsor structure, which is why you'll see that question online. You don't need to introduce anyone to earn on your bookings, and we'll never push you to.

Who can I book for?

Anyone who asks you to plan their travel: followers, friends, family and you. Bookings are made through InteleTravel's partners and booking tools.

Do I need travel-industry experience?

No. InteleTravel provides the training and booking tools. Your travel knowledge and your audience's trust are what you bring.

How much will I earn?

We can't tell you, and you should be wary of anyone who does. It depends entirely on what you book and with whom. The illustration above shows how commission is worked out, not what you'll make.

What does it cost?

There's a joining fee and a monthly fee, both set by InteleTravel. We'll go through the current figures and terms with you on a call so you have the full picture before deciding.

Will it affect my affiliate deals?

It shouldn't replace them, but some brand and network agreements have exclusivity terms. Check yours before you join.
